## 4. What a challenger could plausibly exploit

Evidence-backed, from the two tables above:

1. **Sell timing.** Both agents issue similar order volumes but end at
   different cash, and 59% of head-to-head games are decided by under
   $1,000. Sale timing is where the remaining margin lives.
2. **Terminal liquidation.** The close games are decided at the end of the
   season; unsold inventory is exactly where a few hundred dollars hides.
3. **Opponent-conditioned selling.** 3.1% of worlds flip winner with the
   seat, so the world - and by extension the opponent's revealed
   behaviour - is a real source of information.

Route repair, worker count and livestock mix are NOT supported by this
evidence: both top agents already sit at 41.9% productive action share and
12 hands, and the two lineages reach the same bank within noise.
